## 🔬 Research Interests
My research focuses on bridging the gap between electromagnetic theory and practical wireless systems. Specific areas include:
- **Antenna Design & Optimization:** Monopole antennas, S-Band patch antennas, and compact multi-band structures.
- **THz Photonics & Polarizers:** Nickel wire-grid polarizers, nanoimprint-guided electroforming, and FDTD simulation (MEEP).
- **Wireless Communications:** Diversity techniques (MRC), channel estimation (MMSE), and physical layer reliability.

---

## 👩‍🔬 Biography

I am a third-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student at **SRM Institute of Science and Technology**. My work spans both hardware fabrication and software simulation, with a focus on next-generation electromagnetic devices and reliable wireless communication.

In 2026, I served as a **Research Intern at National Chi Nan University, Taiwan** (TEEP Programme), where I worked under Prof. Vincent K.S. Hsiao. During this time, I simulated nickel wire-grid polarizers using **MEEP** for THz applications and validated experimental data using **Origin Software**.

I am currently a **First Author** on a manuscript regarding monopole antenna enhancement (under review, 2026) and a **Co-Author** on a manuscript regarding additive fabrication of polarizers (under review, *Precision Engineering*, Elsevier).
